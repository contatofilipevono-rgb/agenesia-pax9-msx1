# Figure legends (draft, English)

All figures were generated by `scripts/figuras.py` (matplotlib, 300 dpi PNG + vector PDF). The rule used throughout is DNA contact (< 4 Å) or ΔΔG ≥ 1.5 kcal/mol. The colour palette was checked for colour-vision deficiency (validator: all checks pass).

**Figure 1. Distance of each residue to DNA in AlphaFold 3 models of the PAX9 paired domain (A) and the MSX1 homeodomain (B).**
- Bars show the minimum heavy-atom distance of each residue to DNA in the top-ranked wild-type model. Dark bars mark residues with a pathogenic missense variant. The dashed line marks the 4 Å contact cut-off.
- Dots above the bars represent individual pathogenic variants, stacked when more than one occurs at the same residue. They are coloured by predicted mechanism: DNA contact (blue), destabilizing, ΔΔG ≥ 1.5 kcal/mol without contact (orange), or neither (grey).
- Models: PAX9 residues 6–137 with the DNA from PDB 6PAX; MSX1 residues 166–237 with the DNA from PDB 1IG7. AlphaFold Server, 15 Nov 2026.

**Figure 1C (candidate panel). AlphaFold 3 model of the PAX9 paired domain (residues 6–137) bound to double-stranded DNA.**
- Image exported from the AlphaFold Server viewer (Mol*, Ultra HD, auto-crop), file `Fig1C_PAX9_DNA_AF3_plddt.png` (1728×1340).
- Coloured by per-atom confidence (pLDDT): dark blue > 90, light blue 70–90, yellow 50–70. Almost the whole complex is in the very-high band; only the N-terminal end of the construct is less confident. ipTM 0.86, pTM 0.85.
- *Required notice:* "Structure predicted with AlphaFold Server; use is subject to the AlphaFold Server Output Terms of Use" (Abramson et al., 2024).

**Figure 2. Two complementary mechanisms of pathogenic missense variants.**
- Each point is a pathogenic variant (circles, PAX9, n = 32; squares, MSX1, n = 7), plotted by distance of the mutated residue to DNA (x) and predicted change in folding free energy (ThermoMPNN, y).
- Dashed lines: 4 Å and 1.5 kcal/mol. DNA-contact variants show near-background ΔΔG (PAX9 median 0.49 vs 0.56 for all substitutions). Non-contact variants are destabilizing (median ≈ 1.8; AUC 0.88, p = 0.0003).
- Selected variants are labelled.

**Figure 3. Pathogenic versus population variants in the PAX9 paired domain.**
- (A) Predicted ΔΔG of pathogenic variants (n = 32) and recurrent gnomAD v4 missense variants in the same domain (allele count ≥ 2, n = 48). Horizontal bars show medians (1.17 vs 0.34 kcal/mol; Mann–Whitney p = 4×10⁻⁵).
- (B) Percentage of variants at DNA-contact residues (50% vs 17%; Fisher p = 0.0025), with ΔΔG ≥ 1.5 (41% vs 4%), and meeting either criterion (84% vs 21%).

**Figure 4. Number of missing teeth by predicted mechanism.**
- Number of missing permanent teeth, excluding third molars, reported per PAX9 variant in Intarak et al. (2023), grouped by predicted mechanism. Null variants (frameshift/nonsense) are shown for reference. Bars show medians.
- Mechanism did not predict severity (destabilizing vs contact, p = 0.40). Missense variants were milder than null variants (≈10 vs 15 teeth; AUC 0.77, p = 0.001).

**Figure 5. AlphaGenome 2 versus SpliceAI for non-coding PAX9 and MSX1 variants.**
- Each point is a ClinVar non-coding variant: pathogenic (violet), benign (grey) or VUS (open circles).
- y-axis: maximum AlphaGenome 2 splice-site score across mesenchymal stem cell, neural crest cell and tongue conditions. x-axis: maximum SpliceAI Δ score (Ensembl VEP). Log scales; SpliceAI values < 0.005 are shown at 0.005.
- Both tools separate pathogenic from benign variants perfectly (AUC 1.0). Seven VUS score high in both, including two intronic MSX1 variants (c.470-9G>A and c.470-10C>A).
